Hello! I'm Emily. Here are some quick facts about me:

  • I'm a UX Designer and most recently designed complex cybersecurity enterprise software. My role consisted of end to end design - I love seeing projects through from the beginning research phase to design to dev support.
  • I originally studied mechanical engineering in my undergrad and then transited into UX design in my graduate studies. I enjoy combining my creative and analytical sides to help understand and solve complex problems in my current role.
  • I first discovered UX design while studying abroad in Copenhagen, Denmark. Scandinavian design of products, systems, and spaces was inspiring, and helped me discover the fact that people have careers creating these experiences.
  • In my free time I love to stay active by swimming, playing disc golf, reading, and occasionally creating fun vlogs on Youtube about my life and experiences.
