summary

Research & Discovery

I was tasked with conducting research to discover what we could improve in our analytics area. Since this was very open ended, I opted to conduct a series of interviews with both internal and external users and stakeholders to better understand areas where we could improve and a survey to find out how to improve the main analytics grid management area we knew needed help. The survey was embedded in the analytics grid page via Pendo.

Sample of some survey questions:

  • What are your primary objectives when you navigate to the analytics rule area? (multiple choice selection with option to answer "Other")
  • How helpful would it be to group or bundle rules together to help organize them within the analytics rule area? (Likert scale)
  • What other thoughts or feedback do you have about the analytics rule experience in Axon that you think we should know? (Free text input)

Sample of some interview questions:

  • Can you walk me through your process when creating an analytics rule?
  • How much information do you include in the rule description field? Is this information for admin/rule creators or for analysts?
  • How do you typically search for a specific rule? ​

Results & Application

After conducting four interviews and gathering five survey results, I started distilling data into actionable items. This included uploading and tagging the research results in Dovetail to see patterns and creating a research report with general findings of 11 UX recommendations for improvements and smaller insights related to each recommendation with specific data points that informed it. These findings were presented to the project management team to help inform roadmap items and priority for design and development work.

Sample of some recommendations:

  • Consolidate grid row actions to be consistent across single and bulk action options
  • Have option to export rules grid to help with reporting and understanding coverage
  • Allow users to map fields between rule blocks in the analytics rule builder to expand use case coverage
  • Improve filter query UI to be easier to edit and view in the rule builder ​

Design Improvements

Based on the above research findings, I knew that users struggled to understand what they were creating, needed more guidance, and had issues when working on small screen sizes. In order to address these issues, I focused on my on the following:

  • Use space more efficiently
  • Help users understand what they are building
  • Simplify group bys and complex logic between rule blocks

I began may process with sketching out some general ideas for improved layouts that would help utilize space better and avoid displaying redundant information. This included considering the structure of the page, a user's order of operations when building a rule, and ways to reduce unnecessary clicks.

After sketching, I moved into Figma to create more detailed UI mockups and iterate on designs based on feedback from the project PM, engineers, and other designers.

Final Design

This work concluded with several interviews to validate the final designs with a usability test, where all users were able to successfully complete their tasks. before being handed off to developers for impementation.
