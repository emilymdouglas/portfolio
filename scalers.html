Designing an Intelligence System

My process for designing an intelligence layer to accelerate users via agent suggestions, point in time help, and more.

Research • Systems Design • Product Management • UX Design • UI Design

A common problem users run into when building cloud infrastucture is not knowing how and when to effectively scale their resources to minimize management overhead and costs. This can cause frustration and increases the risk of user churn. To mitigate this, I led an initiative aimed at increasing user retention and satisfaction among growing, high-value users. By leveraging data analysis and targeted research, we identified key churn risks tied to scaling challenges in cloud infrastructure. To help mitigate this, I designed and started implementing a system of personalized, behavior-driven “smart nudges” that provided timely, educational guidance within the platform experience, while also defining orchestration rules to balance relevance and avoid user fatigue. In parallel to this work, I also established a design framework for AI-driven and inference-based experiences across the platform.This work resulted in a 40% lift in resource creation among targeted users for the first initiative and improved the scalability and consistency of intelligent in-product guidance. Interested in learning more about my process for this project? Continue reading for a more in depth explanation.

Research

The first step of this project was to understand our target users better through data analysis. One challenge was determining how to categroize users to create more targeted and relevant experiences. A teammate with much more robust snowflake and SQL skills than I kicked off this project by diving into our data lake to better understand how we might categorize users to then better understand their needs, user journey, pain points, churn risks, etc. An example of how we might categorize users is shown here:

Once we identified these potential user segments, we opted to focus on the largest one as ouor key user segment to target (in this case, users with heavy virtual machine usage) to keep scope manageable and help validate our approach before moving on to target other segments. I started doing my own qualitative research to better understand their needs, pain points they may run into while scaling their infrastructure, and what considerations they may be making. As I don't have a comphresive background as a developer, I used Google Gemini to triage my inital list of questions about these users and the technologies they are using. After this, I also made sure to validate my findings with my own dev team to ensure that we were moving in the right direction. See a sample of this work here. Once this was complete, I started outlining a user journey that our target users may go through, including when and where their pain points are.

Design & Implementation

After we conducted our research, I recommended we create a playbook that suggests personalized nudges based on a user's behavior, their cloud infrastructure, and signals about resource usage and capacity. The nudges would suggest specific actions a user could take to get point in time help to troubleshoot issues or other growing pains they are showing signs of related to scaling their cloud infrastructure. As acting PM for this initiative, I was also responsible for writing up an initial PRD to guide the development of this feature.

I wanted to integrate these smart nudges into the UI to feel seamless and not like a marketing message. To do this, I focused on educational based content with step-by-step instructions for whatever we were suggesting in the nudge and additional information on why this was benefical to the user at this point in their user journey. For example, the smart nudge shown below is shown on a global landing page where users manage their virtual machines. Once a user has more than 3+ VMs, it is generally best practice to leverage a load balancer to decrease management overhead and potential infrastructure risks.

Another consideration included in this work was how often users would encounter these nudges to ensure they were providing value without being intrusive. The aim was to build trust with users through targeted communication, not spam them with several nudges (past research on the effectiveness of banners, pop ups, and other marketing messages showed a strong case of user fatigue from overuse throughout the platform). In order to due this, I created an outline for a nudge orchestration system that helped answer the following:

  • When and where should we show a nudge?
  • If users are eligible for more than one nudge, which one should we prioritize to show?
  • How often should we show nudges?
  • How long is the cool down period once a user interacted with a nudge?

Besides these smart nudges, there was also an ongoing initiative within the company to create more inference based experiences throughout the platform via agents and other AI tools. While we had a very loose design style for these inference-based elements, there was no formal guidance in place for sytling, components, or interaction patterns. How should smart nudges interact with other AI-driven experiences? As we continued to accerlate both the nudge and inference based user experiences, we needed to ensure a cohesive user experience for both styling and interactions.

This led to a tangential workstream focused on creating a more cohesive design language for all AI-driven experiences within the platform. Especially in the AI age we current reside, it is no longer acceptable to have a AI assistance that feels disconnected from the rest of the user experience. During this process, I outlined design principles, interaction patterns, and visual guidelines to ensure consistency across the platform and ensure that it felt cohesive and integrated. This involved collaborating with various other product designers to understand what styling they have used for inference-based experiences in the past, PMs to understand any business requirements other business units may have, and engineers to understand potential technical constraints and existing coded components. Project Outcomes

After launching the nudge, we saw a +40% lift in resource creation for users that were shown the nudge compared to the control group. This was a huge win for our team and showed that we were able to successfully identify a pain point for users and provide them with helpful information at the right time. Additionally, by creating a more cohesive design language for all AI-driven experiences within the platform, we were able to create a more seamless and integrated user experience for our customers, allowing us to more easily scale these expereinces in the future. Finally, a few final learnings from this work included:

  • Better understanding of what it takes to build a roadmap and define team priorities for greenfield work
  • How to leverage AI to help accelerate user research
  • Systems design is much like wrangling cats (good thing I like cats?)

So, what's next? This project helped to prove that validity of our approach of building out a playbook for a certain user segment. However, we only created one nudge, when in reality a system of nudges will be needed for each user segment to cover all users and help accelerate their growth and minimize growing pains. Future work should include a continuation of building out the nudge and orchestration system, with more research done to hone in on placement and timing to ensure a seamless and helpful user experience.
